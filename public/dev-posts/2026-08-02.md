## Player Limits per Server

- Added response limits for every section.
- **Classic** = 100 players for all sections.
- **Seasonal** = 500 players for all sections.

> _**Seasonal** vault is designed to handle much larger data volumes due to their finite retention window.  
> **Classic** vault however, require more data curation because its meant for long-term storage, and retaining unnecessary data can lead to avoidable storage costs._

## Deaths Section

- **PvP/PvE breakdown** — the Total Deaths card now shows a visual split of PvP vs PvE deaths with counts and percentages.

## Data & Time Ranges

- **Always up to date** — Sections now fetch the latest data every time you open them, so you never see stale numbers until manually refreshing.
- **Timezone-friendly ranges** — Day, week, month and year ranges are now calculated against the latest available data and stay consistent no matter your timezone.

## UI/UX Improvements

- **Badges** – Applied to 10h+ and 14h+ on online activity, replacing the old full row colors.
- **Colors** — The page colors have been updated.

## Fixes & Optimizations

- **Achievements** — Fixed magic and skill milestones not rendering correctly sometimes.
- Minor optimizations under the hood.
