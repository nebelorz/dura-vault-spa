## Deaths Section

A brand-new section dedicated to tracking deaths across the server.

- **Death log** with filterable list — browse all deaths for the selected period with PvP/PvE toggle.
- **Stat cards** showing Total Deaths, PvP Top Killer, PvE Top Killer, and Highest Level Death.
- **Interactive charts**: deaths per day timeline, top PvP killers, top PvE killers, and PvP vs PvE distribution.

Data is collected daily from the official Dura character pages and available for Day, Week, Month, Year, and All-time periods.
