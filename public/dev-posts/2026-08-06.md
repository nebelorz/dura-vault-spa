## UI/UX Improvements

- **Colors** — The main colors across the app have been changed.

## Player Details

- **Performance Chart** — The performance chart has been re-worked. Lines can be enabled/disabled as you like, using the summary buttons on its top.
- **Active Period** — Now shows only the days you actually gained XP, level, or rank, instead of long flat stretches between changes. Also affects AVGs.
- **Info Tooltip** — The icon next to the period selector now explains what each period displays.

## Fixes & Optimizations

- **Performance** — The app now loads more quickly.
- Minor optimizations under the hood.
