## Complete Data for Every Period

- **No more caps** — Deaths, Top Gainers, and Online Activity now load ALL the available entries for the selected period, instead of being cut off at a fixed number of rows.
- **Applies to every range** — Whether you pick a day, a week, a month, or a year, you get the full picture for that period.
- **Consistent across servers** — The same unlimited results now apply to both Classic and Seasonal (Aetas).

## Clearer Errors & Retry

- **Error messages** — If a section or a player page fails to load, you now see a clear message telling you something went wrong, instead of it just looking like there is no data.
- **Retry button** — Press Retry to try loading the data again right away, without leaving the page.

## Notifications

- **Position** — pop-up messages now appear in the bottom-right corner of the screen, so they no longer cover the top of the page.
- **Looks** — notifications have a softer styling.

## Fixes & Optimizations

- Minor optimizations under the hood.
