## Multi-Server Support

The app now supports both **Classic** and **Seasonal** servers.

- **Server toggle** in the navigation bar — switch between Classic and Seasonal with one click.
- **Dual data sources** — the app loads data from the selected server's database and official character pages.
- **Reactive reloading** — all sections (highscores, online activity, deaths, landing page) automatically refresh when you switch servers.
- **Server external links** — All official Dura links points to the selected server's homepage.

## Deaths Section

A brand-new section dedicated to tracking deaths across the server.

- **Death log** with filterable list — browse all deaths for the selected period with PvP/PvE toggle.
- **Stat cards** showing Total Deaths, PvP/PvE Top Killer.
- **Charts, charts, charts!** deaths per day timeline, top PvP killers, top PvE killers, and PvP vs PvE distribution.

Data is collected daily from the official Dura character pages and available for Day, Week, Month, Year, and All-time periods.

## UI/UX Improvements

- **New badge system** — consistent badge variants across player lists.
- **Expanded color system** — additional colors for better theming support.
- **Unified** font weights.

## Fixes & Optimizations

- Code cleanup and minor refactoring across multiple components.
